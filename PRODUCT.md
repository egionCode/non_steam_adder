# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PC Gamers and Steam users who maintain a collection of DRM-free or non-Steam games and want them to feel like first-class citizens in their Steam Library.

## Product Purpose
A specialized desktop utility that automates the tedious process of adding non-Steam games to the Steam client. It handles AppID generation, VDF manipulation, and automatically sources/applies official high-resolution artwork from SteamGridDB.

## Brand Personality
**Functional, Integrated, Reliable.**
The app should feel like a power-user extension of the Steam client itself—no unnecessary flair, just high utility and seamless integration with the existing ecosystem.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered "multi-tool" launchers with excessive animations.
- Generic Electron-style apps that ignore platform/Steam aesthetics.
- Manual multi-step wizards that could be automated.

## Design Principles
- **Steam Synergy:** Look and feel like an organic part of the Steam interface to reduce cognitive load.
- **Automated-First:** Prefer extracting metadata and guessing titles over asking the user for input.
- **Visual Fidelity:** Prioritize the display and selection of high-quality artwork as the core value proposition.
- **Surgical Utility:** Perform one task exceptionally well and then get out of the way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text (Steam-inspired palette).
- Support for keyboard navigation in image selection galleries.
- Clear visual cues for drag-and-drop operations.
